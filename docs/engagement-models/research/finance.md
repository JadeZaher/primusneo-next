## 1. Comparable Vendor Pricing

### Financial Data Fabrics / Middleware

Bloomberg Terminal: **$31,980/seat/year** (single seat); **$28,320/seat/year** (multi-seat); negotiated enterprise at **$18,000–$20,000/seat** (50+ seats). Hardware + add-ons push true Y1 cost to $34K–$35.5K/seat. Refinitiv Eikon (LSEG) ~**$22,000/seat/year** full feed; stripped variants from $3,600/year; exchange pass-through adds 20–50%. FactSet ~**$12,000/seat/year** mid-range. Snowflake / Databricks: **$25K–$40K/year** for 200 TB analytics; **$2M–$10M/year** for 500-analyst enterprise footprints.

### Tokenization Platforms

Securitize: minimum engagement **$50K–$100K** (setup + issuance bundled); ongoing compliance/transfer-agent annual retainers. Tokeny (T-REX, EU-focused): SaaS licensing plus setup, comparable to Securitize. Polymath/Polymesh: transparent on-chain transaction fees. TaaS SMB SaaS: **$2K–$5K/month** (~$24K–$60K/year). White-label platforms: **$50K–$150K** upfront; custom development from $150K.

For live issuance: $3M offering = **$75K–$200K** all-in; $30M offering = **$200K–$1M+**. Platforms charge **0.5%–2.5%** of asset-under-tokenization for smart-contract deployment + compliance wrapping; success-fee models charge **6%–10%** of funds raised + 2% equity allocation. Onyx (now Kinexys) by JPM and Provenance are relationship-gated.

### Custody / Wallet-as-a-Service

| Provider | Model | Rate |
|----------|-------|------|
| BitGo | AUM bps | 5 bps/month (60 bps/yr) on AUC >$100K |
| Fireblocks | API-usage + bespoke infra | Negotiated; ~$1M+ AUC minimum |
| Anchorage | Bilateral negotiation | $10M+ AUC minimum |

### Compliance / KYC / AML

Chainalysis Reactor: ~**$40K/year** single-seat; enterprise KYT: **$100K–$500K+/year**. Elliptic Forensics: comparable to Chainalysis at enterprise. TRM Labs: modular chain-coverage; negotiated. ComplyAdvantage: **$500–$5K/year** SMB API; enterprise negotiated.

### Governance / GRC

Diligent, Nasdaq Boardvantage: custom enterprise; Vendr shows Fortune 500 negotiate **30–50% off** initial quotes. ServiceNow IRM/GRC: Fortune 500 negotiate **60–80% off list**; mandatory **5–10% annual uplift** since 2024.

## 2. Engagement Scale Tiers — 3-Year TCO

| Tier | Profile | 3-Year TCO |
|------|---------|-----------|
| **Small** | Boutique manager / family office <$1B AUM | **$350K–$900K** |
| **Mid** | Regional bank or asset manager $1–50B AUM | **$1.5M–$5M** |
| **Large** | G-SIB, top-20 asset manager, major exchange | **$8M–$25M+** |

**Small:** TaaS SaaS ($72K–$180K), legal/structuring ($100K–$250K), custody ~60 bps/yr on $100M AUC, KYC/AML ($30K–$90K), governance software ($30K–$60K), integration/dev ($75K–$150K).

**Mid:** White-label tokenization ($150K–$500K), issuance legal ($200K–$600K), Fireblocks/BitGo custody on $500M–$2B notional, Chainalysis ($150K–$300K), GRC/IRM ($300K–$600K), data fabric ($150K–$400K), Big-4 SI ($500K–$1.5M).

**Large:** Custom tokenization + interop fabric ($2M–$5M build), multi-custodian, Snowflake/Databricks at scale ($2M–$10M/yr), full Chainalysis + TRM ($400K–$1M/yr), ServiceNow IRM ($600K–$1.5M/yr), Big-4 transformation ($3M–$10M).

## 5. Regulatory Overlay

**MiCA (EU, fully applicable Dec 2024):** CASP licensing build-out €50K–€100K (small); capital reqs €50K (advisory), €125K (custody/exchange), €150K (trading platforms). Non-compliance fines: €15M or 3% of revenue. Creates direct demand for immutable governance logs, auditable issuance records, automated sanctions screening, cross-border identity portability.

**OCC Interpretive Letter 1184 (May 2025):** National banks may provide crypto custody (fiduciary + non-fiduciary), use sub-custodians, but must demonstrate controls. Banks will pay **$200K–$500K+/year** for platforms that produce attestations on-demand.

**SEC SAB 122 (Jan 2025):** Rescinds SAB 121 on-balance-sheet liability — removes primary accounting disincentive for bank custody.

**FCA (UK):** Implicit compliance tax **£100K–£500K/year** in incremental monitoring/reporting under crypto-asset registration and stablecoin frameworks.

**Aggregate signal:** Auditability + immutable governance logs + automated attestations are highest-value features FIs pay a premium for — they directly reduce regulatory capital risk and legal liability.

|------|--------------|---------------|----------------|-------|
| Small | CIO / COO | CTO | — | 2–4 mo |
| Mid | Head of Digital Assets | CDO / CTO | CRO | 6–12 mo |

**Mandatory mid/large requirements:** SOC 2 Type II (US default), ISO 27001 (EU/APAC), annual 3rd-party pen test, BCP/DR docs, GDPR/data residency, reference clients in regulated FS. Missing SOC 2 or ISO 27001 disqualifies at mid/large.

## 7. Rejection Reasons

1. **Regulatory ambiguity** — Legal/compliance veto until local regulator gives written guidance.
2. **Custody model mismatch** — Must map to self-custody MPC/HSM, qualified custodian, or bank-grade sub-custodian.
3. **Legacy core-banking integration gap** — Temenos/Finastra/Murex/Calypso require certified FIX/API connectors.
4. **Open-source / IP ownership risk** — Unaudited smart contracts + no formal vuln disclosure + no IP indemnity = reject.
5. **Vendor concentration / lock-in risk** — Multi-custodian, multi-chain is a requirement at G-SIB.
6. **Missing security certs** — SOC 2 + ISO 27001 non-negotiable at mid/large.

---

## Summary

FIs deploying tokenization, governance, and interop infrastructure face: Bloomberg ($32K/seat) and Refinitiv ($22K/seat) anchor information-layer reference price; tokenization platforms (Securitize, Tokeny) charge $50K–$150K setup + 0.5%–2.5% of issuance value; custody (BitGo, Fireblocks, Anchorage) at 5–60 bps/year on AUC. 3-year TCO: $350K (boutique) to $25M+ (G-SIB). Big-4 day rates: $2K–$10K. MiCA, OCC Letter 1184, SAB 122 make auditability + immutable governance logs binary procurement requirements. Primary deal-killers: regulatory ambiguity, absence of SOC 2 Type II / ISO 27001, inability to integrate with legacy core-banking. Sales cycles: 6–12 mo at mid, 12–24 mo at G-SIB.
