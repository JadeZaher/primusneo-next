# Layer Comparables — Cross-Cutting Market Research

> **Purpose:** Pricing benchmarks for the four PrimusNeo product layers (AMP, NEOS, AZOA, Fractal Engine) to anchor the value-based commercial pricing model.
> **Date:** 2026-05-23

---

## Layer 1 (AMP) — Spatial OS / 3D Collaboration

### Pricing Benchmarks

**NVIDIA Omniverse Enterprise**
- **$4,500 per GPU per year** (unit is the GPU running the simulation server, not named seat)
- Small studio: 2–4 GPUs = $9K–$18K/yr for compute licensing alone, before add-ons

**Unity Industry / Industrial Collection**
- **$4,950/seat/yr** (post-2025 price revision; was ~$4,500)
- Includes Pixyz, Unity Mars, Unity Cloud, DevOps tooling
- Unity Enterprise (non-industry): ~$2,200/seat/yr after 8% increase effective Jan 2025

**Unreal Engine Enterprise**
- **$1,850/seat/yr** for enterprise seat license — notably lower than Unity, positioned aggressively for industrial/simulation deals

**Spatial.io**
- Business tier (up to 50 users/server, 20 servers, analytics): public listing but $ behind sales conversation
- Enterprise tier (1,000+ concurrent, custom games/XR, dedicated support, advanced security): custom
- **Mozilla Hubs shut down May 31, 2024** — no longer a comparable

**Improbable / Hadean**
- Both pivot to defense simulation + game multiplayer infra
- Public pricing not disclosed; enterprise engagements are **six-figure minimums**

### Service Multiplier
- Typical **1.5–3x** license (heavy SI deployment)

## Layer 2 (NEOS) — Governance / Councils / Holonic Orgs

### Pricing Benchmarks

**Aragon / DAOhaus / Snapshot / Tally** — All free or near-free at baseline:
- **Aragon**: no subscription; L2 deployment gas $50–$200 one-time; custom smart contract buildouts $20K–$100K+ if bespoke
- **DAOhaus**: entirely free, OSS, forkable
- **Snapshot**: free for all off-chain voting; powers 96% of DAOs + 35,000+ communities; gas-free by design
- **Tally**: free self-serve Governor deployer; enterprise/white-glove DAO launch via biz@tally.xyz (no public pricing)

**Diligent Boards / Nasdaq Boardvantage**
- **Diligent**: $12K–$60K+/yr depending on board size, features, org type. Per Vendr, 20–30% discounts routinely negotiated on multi-year
- **Nasdaq Boardvantage**: starting ~$12K–$15K/yr for small boards
- **OnBoard**: affordable alt; similarly quote-based, often 20–40% below Diligent list

**Microsoft Viva**
- Employee Communications & Communities: **$2.00/user/mo**
- Workplace Analytics & Employee Feedback: **$6.00/user/mo**
- Viva Suite (full): **$12.00/user/mo** ($144/seat/yr)

**Loomio**
- Per-group subscription model (not per-seat)
- Nonprofit/cooperative discount; enterprise/custom domain custom-quoted
- Indicative: **$99–$599/month** per organization depending on member count

### Service Multiplier
- Typical **0.5–1x** license (lighter services, mostly config/training)

## Layer 3 (AZOA) — Interoperability Fabric

### Pricing Benchmarks

**MuleSoft Anypoint Platform**
- **$1,250/vCore/month** on-demand
- Min production deployment ~1 vCore = $15K/yr entry, but real enterprise starts **$80K–$250K+/yr**
- Large enterprise: commonly **$500K–$2M+/yr** under multi-year ELA
- Services-to-license: **2–3x Year 1** (impl, DataWeave dev, architecture)
- Total Y1 TCO mid-market: $350K–$600K+

**Boomi**
- Professional ~$550/month ($6,600/yr) for lower volumes
- Mid-market: $30K–$150K/yr
- Enterprise: six figures custom
- Services multiple: **1–2x** vs MuleSoft's 2–3x

**Workato**
- Entry: ~$10K/yr (SMB automation)
- Enterprise: 20–30% lower than MuleSoft for equivalent volumes ($60K–$1.5M+ depending on recipe volume)
- Services: 1–2x license

**Blockchain interoperability (Chainlink CCIP, LayerZero, Axelar, Wormhole)**
- None publish per-tx list prices; costs denominated in gas + relayer fees (network-condition-dependent)
- Axelar processed $8.66B of transfers + 1.85M transactions by May 2024 across 64 chains
- Enterprise SLA (e.g., Chainlink BUILD) negotiated directly
- Cost advantage flows to LayerZero + Wormhole for high-frequency use cases; CCIP holds mid-cost position with stronger security guarantees

**Decentralized storage (Pinata / Web3.Storage)**
- Pinata: **~$0.15/GB/month** on paid plans; enterprise custom
- Web3.Storage: free tier wound down; now enterprise-focused custom SLAs
- Ceramic (identity/data streams): no public pricing; developer-tier free on hosted node; self-hosted OSS

### Service Multiplier
- **2–3x Year 1** (connector buildout, architecture); 0.5–1x maintenance from Y2

## Layer 4 (Fractal Engine) — Intelligence / Analytics / Operational Telemetry

### Pricing Benchmarks

**Palantir Foundry**
- No public per-seat price. Enterprise ACV: **$1M–$10M+/yr** for gov; commercial mid-market entry $250K–$500K/yr
- Dimensions: compute-seconds + storage (GB-months) + Ontology (GB-months) + named-user software licenses bundled into ELA
- **Gross margin: 80.25% (FY2024)** on combined platform
- Services-to-license: bundles O&M services as single performance obligation with software. Pre-2022 service-heavy model ran close to 1:1 software-to-services; early Palantir gov contracts ran **3–5x services vs license** in total deal value — a model they are deliberately shrinking

**Snowflake**
- Compute: $2–$4/credit on-demand; $1.50–$2.50/credit under committed-use
- Storage: $23/TB/month (AWS US regions)
- Enterprise commits: $250K–$1M+ to unlock meaningful credit discounts
- **Gross margin: ~75–78%** software

**Databricks**
- DBUs: $0.15–$0.40/DBU (Enterprise tier includes Unity Catalog + advanced governance)
- Cloud compute billed separately (dual-bill model)
- Storage: ~$0.02/GB/month on underlying object storage
- 700+ customers spending $1M+/yr; net dollar retention >140%; annualized revenue $5.4B (Jan 2026)
- **Gross margin: ~74–80%** (declining slightly as AI workloads increase compute intensity)
- Services largely delivered through SI partners; Databricks direct services multiple: **0.3–0.5x**

**Splunk**
- On-premises: ~$150/GB/day/year list; enterprise security (ES) $250–$400/GB/day
- Cloud premium: 1.2–1.5x on-premises rates
- Multi-year discounts: 10–20%
- Mid-size (200 hosts, 500 GB/day): **$30K–$60K/month** under enterprise agreement

**Datadog**
- Logs ingest: **$0.10/GB ingest + $1.70/M indexed**
- 200 GB/day at list exceeds $1.3M/yr for log mgmt alone
- Hosts (infra monitoring): ~$15–$23/host/month
- Mid-size (200 hosts + 500 GB logs + APM): **$15K–$25K/month**

**C3.ai / Cognite Data Fusion / AspenTech**
- All enterprise custom-quote, no disclosed per-seat list
- C3.ai reported $108.7M in FY2025 Q4 revenue with consumption-based model
- Cognite Data Fusion on Azure Marketplace with custom quotes; heavy-industry OT/IT convergence target
- AspenTech remains industrial simulation/optimization benchmark, similarly opaque

### Service Multiplier
- **1–2x Year 1** (data pipeline buildout, model training, ontology mapping); 0.3–0.5x from Y2

## OSS-to-Paid Conversion Playbook

**Relevant precedents for NEOS (CC-NC) + Fractal (CC-NC) + AZOA SDK:**

- **MongoDB SSPL** (2018): Switched from AGPL to SSPL after AWS launched DocumentDB. SSPL forces SaaS providers offering MongoDB as a service to open their entire stack — effectively a commercial license requirement for cloud deployments. MongoDB gross margin ~73–75% on software.
- **Elastic (ELv2 + SSPL then AGPL)**: Moved from Apache 2.0 to dual SSPL+ELv2 in 2021 after AWS forked OpenSearch; returned to AGPL Sep 2024. **Lesson: CC-NC/SSPL-style restrictions stop cloud hyperscaler free-riding but must have a clean commercial escape valve.**
- **HashiCorp BSL** (Aug 2023, now IBM/HashiCorp): Switched Terraform, Vault, Consul from MPL 2.0 to BSL 1.1. BSL allows production use except by direct competitors; converts to OSI-recognized license after a defined "Change Date." **Closest precedent to PrimusNeo's CC-NC**: non-competing entities get near-open access; commercial SaaS competitors must buy a license.
- **Conversion rate reality**: OSS-to-paid in infra software runs **1–5% of active community users**. At 10,000 active NEOS deployments → realistic paid conversion 100–500 orgs. Revenue-per-converted-customer must be high enough to sustain dev — typically **$10K–$100K+/yr** for enterprise governance tooling.

## Outcome / Value-Based Pricing Precedents

- **Zendesk AI agents (2024)**: Switched to outcomes-based — charges only for successful AI resolutions, not seat licenses or consumption
- **Intercom Resolution Bot**: Pay per successful resolution, zero cost for failed interactions
- **Gartner forecast**: **30%+ of enterprise SaaS will incorporate outcome-based components by 2025** (up from ~15%)
- **McKinsey**: Value-based pricing companies achieve profit margins 15% above peers
- **Palantir**: Early gov contracts included value-share clauses (% fraud recovered, % logistics savings). Materially harder to audit and collect on; Palantir moved away post-2018
- **ServiceNow pilots**: Outcome pricing with guaranteed efficiency improvement SLAs — full rate only when targets met

## Gross Margin Targets

Comparable software gross margins: Palantir 80%, Snowflake 75–78%, Databricks 74–80%, MuleSoft (within Salesforce) ~73–78%. **PrimusNeo target blended gross margin on license/subscription revenue: 72–80%** — achievable given the federated steward payout structure (stewards receive a share of license revenue, which is the primary cost-of-revenue). Services gross margin target: **35–50%** (consistent with mid-tier SIs). **Blended target at scale: 65–72%**.
