## 4. Pricing Mechanics — Industry Norms

- **Per-shipment:** $0.50–$10 (CargoWise $6–$10 anchor); TMS, T&T, eBL
- **Per-TEU/container:** Software-rare; carrier/terminal surcharges (THC). Twin/analytics experiments per-vessel-call $200–$2K
- **Per-vehicle/per-asset:** Fleet telematics $10–$30/vehicle/mo SaaS GPS; $15–$50/tracker/mo for IoT
- **Per-warehouse/per-DC:** WMS SaaS $3K–$10K/site/mo + per-user + per-tx tiers; multi-DC discounts standard
- **% of freight spend:** Managed transport (4PL/TMSaaS): 0.5%–2%
- **Per-API-call:** Emerging for carrier data + port community; $0.001–$0.05/call at vol; project44 carrier network access partly metered this way
- **Annual platform + overage:** Dominant mid-market model

**PrimusNeo positioning:** AZOA priced per-API-call/per-integration-event (mirroring MuleSoft/Boomi at $0.01–$0.10/tx). NEOS governance priced per-entity (carrier, 3PL, shipper) — network-effect pricing rewarding ecosystem growth.

## 5. Regulatory Overlay

**IMO CII / EEXI (in force Jan 2023):** Vessels ≥5K GT must calculate/report CII annually. D-rated 3 consecutive years or E → corrective action plan. CII tightens ~2%/yr through 2026. Drives demand for voyage optimization, slow-steaming analytics — direct Fractal Engine use case.

**EU ETS — Maritime (2024 onward):** 100% of intra-EU + 50% of extra-EU voyages calling EU ports. At ~€60–70/tonne CO₂, compliance costs reach tens of millions €/yr for large carrier — hard financial incentive for AMP-driven route/speed optimization.

**EU CBAM (definitive phase Jan 2026):** Authorized declarants only for steel, aluminium, cement, fertilizers, electricity, hydrogen. CBAM certificates tied to EU ETS price. Creates new doc workflows + customs classification + supplier carbon data — addressable by NEOS + AZOA.

**US 21st Century Customs Framework (21CCF):** Mandatory e-record-keeping, expanded SC data sharing, ACE 2.0 (in progress). Customs brokers must report data breaches. AZOA positioned for 21CCF-compliant data flows.

**Single Window Mandates:** IMO FAL + EU Maritime Single Window framework (effective 2025) require pre-arrival + port clearance data via national single-window — AZOA integration play at port-operator + shipping-line tier.

## 6. Procurement Signals

| Buyer | Cycle |
|-------|-------|
| **CSCO / CPO** | Large enterprise; 9–18 mo. 2024 CSCO agenda: resilience + tech ROI + sustainability |
| **COO / Head of Network Ops** | Mid-tier 3PLs / regional carriers; 6–12 mo |
| **CTO / VP Engineering** | Technical gatekeeper for API platforms; critical for AZOA/NEOS pilot approval |
| **Head of Trade Compliance / VP Customs** | NEOS + CBAM/21CCF tooling buyer |

**Carrier–shipper–3PL triangle:** No single party controls the network. Carriers own vessel data; 3PLs own last-mile/warehouse data; shippers own cargo data. TradeLens failed precisely because Maersk tried to own a network requiring trust from its competitors. **PrimusNeo's governance-neutral NEOS positioning is a direct answer.**

**RFP cycles:** Top-20 carriers + top-5 3PLs run formal tech RFPs annually/biannually; typically Q1 for budget alignment. Target Q3–Q4 budget season convos.

## 7. Rejection Reasons

**Consortium / network-effect fatigue (TradeLens precedent):** TradeLens shut down Nov 2022 after 4 yrs + $1B+ combined investment. Maersk ownership perceived competitively by rivals (MSC, CMA CGM, Evergreen never joined). Asian carriers cited governance concerns. **Lesson for PrimusNeo:** NEOS must demonstrate governance neutrality + open standards alignment (DCSA, GS1, OpenAPI) from day one. No single carrier may access competitor data.

**Legacy TMS/WMS integration complexity:** Oracle OTM, SAP TM, Manhattan have deeply customized large-account implementations. Pre-built connectors required. **Action:** AZOA must publish connector libraries for top-5 TMS + top-5 WMS before enterprise sales scale.

**Carrier data hoarding:** Ocean carriers treat real-time vessel/container position as competitive. p44/FourKites spent years building carrier network agreements; gaps remain. Either partner with existing visibility networks or offer carriers a data-value-exchange model.

**Perceived overlap with existing visibility spend:** Shippers paying $300K+/yr to p44/FourKites won't add another layer. Must displace OR offer superset (spatial context + governance + intelligence).

**"We built it ourselves" at top carriers:** Maersk (Captain), CMA CGM (CEVA), MSC (MSC One), DHL (myDHLi), UPS. Motion here is co-sell / embed (white-label Fractal or AZOA data fabric), not displacement.

## Summary

Logistics tech segments: TMS ($400K–$3M/yr enterprise), WMS ($500K–$3M Y1), visibility ($100K–$1M/yr), port/terminal OS ($300K–$1.5M/yr terminal). Consulting via Accenture/Deloitte/GEP at $1.5K–$8.5K/day depending on seniority. Pricing favors per-shipment ($0.50–$10), per-site, or annual platform; API/event-metered emerging. Regulatory pressure from IMO CII, EU ETS, EU CBAM (now definitive), US 21CCF creates genuine urgency for data-layer + compliance tooling. Primary buyers: CSCOs + COOs at 3PLs/carriers; CTOs hold integration veto. Central challenge: the TradeLens shadow — any cross-party logistics network play must demonstrate ironclad governance neutrality (NEOS), pre-certified integrations (AZOA), and carrier-data value exchange (Fractal) before procurement commits.
