## 2. Engagement Scale Tiers — 3-Year TCO

### Small: GC/Sub <$50M Revenue, 1–2 Active Projects
Procore entry ($5K/yr) + ACC Build (5 seats, ~$8K/yr) + Fieldwire Pro (5 seats, ~$3K/yr) + Matterport Starter. Impl: self-serve or 1–2 SI days; $5K–$15K. **3-yr TCO: $40K–$90K.**
PrimusNeo entry: AZOA as Procore/ACC connector eliminating duplicate entry; AMP for as-built spatial record without displacing incumbents.

### Mid: Regional Contractor/Developer $50–500M, 5–30 Projects
Procore ($25K–$80K/yr) + ACC BIM Collaborate 20–50 seats ($14K–$35K/yr) + CDE Asite/Newforma ($20K–$80K/yr) + DroneDeploy Advanced ($8K/yr) + One Click LCA Designer ($20K/yr). Impl: SI partner 60–120 days; $50K–$150K. **3-yr TCO: $350K–$800K.**
PrimusNeo play: NEOS for cross-project governance/approvals; Fractal replacing PowerBI/Excel portfolio dashboards; AZOA federating CDE + PM + BIM data.

### Large: ENR Top-100, Mega-Projects ($1B+ capex), National Portfolio
Procore enterprise ($200K–$500K/yr) + Bentley iTwin or ACC enterprise ($200K–$1M/yr) + Willow Twin or Honeywell Forge ($250K–$1M/yr) + One Click LCA Power Pack ($80K–$120K/yr) + AECOM/Arup digital advisory ($500K–$2M/yr). Impl: 6–18 mo, $500K–$3M. **3-yr TCO: $3M–$12M.**
PrimusNeo play: Full AMP+NEOS+AZOA+Fractal as federated intelligence/governance layer across portfolio.

## 4. Pricing Mechanics

- **Per-project:** Reality capture (DroneDeploy, Matterport, Open Space), LCA scans (Tally per model), CDE project modules. $500/project (residential) to $50K+ (mega-project)
- **Per-seat/per-user:** Dominant for PM + CDE. Fieldwire $54–$94/user/mo; Aconex $29–$49/user/mo; ACC $42–$135/user/mo by module. Vol discounts at 5–25 seats
- **Per-square-foot (FM):** IWMS + twin licensing **$0.10–$0.50/sf/yr** for portfolios >500K sf. TRIRIGA, Planon, Willow Twin
- **% of Annual Construction Volume:** Procore proprietary; **0.1–0.3% of ACV**. Healthy owner digital program budgets run 1–2% of total capex (McKinsey)
- **% of capex (advisory):** AECOM + Arup digital retainers on mega-projects **0.2–0.5% of capex**. McKinsey Capital Projects 0.5–1% for strategic programs

## 5. Regulatory Overlay

**EU EPBD (recast 2024):** All new buildings must meet Zero-Emission Building criteria. Fossil-fuel boiler subsidies ended Jan 2025. **Digital Building Passports** (per-building records covering energy, materials, lifecycle) mandated as part of EPBD impl. Immediate demand for data fabrics ingesting heterogeneous building system data — **direct AZOA + AMP use case**.

**California Buy Clean Act (BCCA) / AB 2446:** GWP limits on structural steel, concrete rebar, flat glass, insulation on CA public works enforceable Jan 2025. AB 2446 targets 40% reduction in material carbon intensity by 2035. Drives One Click LCA + EC3; Fractal opportunity for automated EPD ingestion + real-time compliance reporting.

**UK Net Zero Carbon Buildings Standard (NZCBS):** v1 launches early 2026, integrating with PAS 2080. Upfront carbon (A1–A5) limits per m². From 2025, publicly funded projects must be designed without fossil-fuel heating.

**ISO 19650 BIM:** Global info-management benchmark; mandatory EU + UK (since 2016), increasingly GCC + India. Part 6 (H&S integration) released 2025. Requires structured CDE — **AMP can serve as the ISO 19650-compliant spatial information layer**.

**LEED / BREEAM / Green Star:** Embodied carbon docs, energy modeling, operational data submissions tied to credit thresholds → ongoing data obligations through bldg ops, supporting persistent AMP/Fractal usage post-construction.

## 6. Procurement Signals

**Primary champions:**
- **VDC / BIM Manager** at GC or design-build: controls day-to-day platform selection for ACC, Procore, CDE. Pain: design-side BIM vs field-side PM interop gaps; duplicate model mgmt
- **CIO / CDO** at ENR-100 contractor: owns enterprise arch + vendor consolidation. Budget authority $500K+. Receptive to consolidation reducing vendor sprawl
- **Project Director / Owner's Rep** on capital programs: schedule + cost + risk visibility across 10–50 active projects. Values federated data without forcing subs onto single mandated tool — **AZOA value prop**
- **Director of Sustainability / ESG Officer**: owns embodied carbon reporting under EPBD/BCCA/NZCBS. Needs automated EPD ingestion + LCA workflow

**RFP trigger events:**
- Owner-mandated ISO 19650 BIM/CDE reqs on public infra tenders (Highways England, GSA, state DOTs, Network Rail)
- Post-merger portfolio consolidation
- Mega-project mobilization: capital programs >$500M trigger digital delivery planning phase
- ESG reporting deadline (annual sustainability report, EU SFDR for EU real estate funds, SEC climate disclosure for US-listed owners)

## 7. Rejection Reasons

**Paper/email-based culture:** Significant portion of workforce (esp subs, trades) on paper, WhatsApp, email. Adoption requires change mgmt buyers consistently underestimate. Sub resistance to yet-another-login is a top-3 Procore complaint.

**Subcontractor adoption friction:** ENR-100 GCs routinely report sub networks won't adopt owner-mandated platforms. Solutions requiring zero friction on subs (read-only views, email-triggered approvals, no account creation) consistently outperform full-onboarding.

**ROI attribution difficulty:** Each construction project is unique → no A/B comparison. Decision-makers struggle to prove platform caused schedule improvement vs weather or skilled superintendent. Vendor-published ROI reports (Procore, Autodesk) discounted by CFOs.

**Integration lock-in:** Most ENR contractors have Procore + ACC + CDE locked in by multi-year contracts. Adding PrimusNeo requires clear AZOA story: bidirectional Procore webhook, IFC/RVT ingestion from ACC, write-back without breaking workflows. **Integration tax = primary reason pilots stall at POC.**

**Price sensitivity on thin margins:** GC net margins 2–4%. Premium add-on without near-term measurable ROI (rework reduction, fewer RFIs, faster financial closeout) gets deferred. Pilot pricing with clear documented-ROI path is table stakes.

## Summary

Construction/AEC is a $16T global market with fragmented multi-vendor software spend and accelerating regulatory pressure on data transparency. Dominant pricing: per-seat ($29–$135/user/mo) and ACV-% (0.1–0.3%) for PM, shifting to per-sf ($0.10–$0.50/sf/yr) for FM digital twins. 3-yr TCO: $40K–$90K (small GC) → $3M–$12M (ENR-100 portfolio). EU EPBD digital building passports + CA Buy Clean Act + UK NZCBS + ISO 19650 directly validate AZOA interop + AMP spatial record propositions. Primary champions: VDC/BIM Managers + CDOs at large contractors; Owner's Reps drive top-down mandates on capital programs. Most durable rejection risks: subcontractor adoption friction, ROI attribution opacity, Procore/Autodesk integration lock-in. PrimusNeo addresses with zero-friction AZOA connectors, documented project-level ROI instrumentation, and pilot structure that proves value in single project lifecycle before portfolio expansion.
