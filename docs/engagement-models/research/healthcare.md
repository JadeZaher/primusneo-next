## 4. Pricing Mechanics

**Per-bed** standard for inpatient EHR ($500–$1,000/bed upfront). **Per-provider/user** for ambulatory/analytics ($25–$3,500/provider/mo). **PMPM** dominant for payer analytics/population health/VBC ($0.50–$200 PMPM by intensity). **Per-API-call** for FHIR fabric/interop. **Enterprise license (ELA)** for broad deployments (Veeva, Datavant). **Per-encounter** emerging for ambient AI (Nuance DAX, Suki, Epic ambient). PrimusNeo positioning: AMP on per-bed/per-facility ELA · AZOA on per-API/flat interop ELA · NEOS on per-covered-life/enterprise governance ELA · Fractal on PMPM/per-research-query.

## 5. Regulatory Overlay

**HIPAA / HITECH:** Legal floor. BAAs non-negotiable. HIPAA Security Rule overhaul (effective May 2026) mandates MFA, universal PHI encryption, 24-hr breach notification, annual pen testing.

**HITRUST CSF:** De facto procurement gate. **81% of hospitals and 83% of payers** require HITRUST from tech vendors. **i1 (~$70K)** is now minimum for most health system RFPs; **r2 ($100K–$500K, 12–15 mo)** expected for core platforms. 3-yr enterprise cycles: $400K–$800K. PrimusNeo must achieve at least HITRUST i1 before substantive RFPs.

**ONC TEFCA / QHIN:** Common Agreement v2.1 effective Jan 2025; 21,086 orgs live. QHIN designation: ~12 months. AZOA as TEFCA-native fabric is a direct differentiator vs Redox/Health Gorilla.

**EU EHDS (Reg EU 2025/327):** In force March 2025; general application March 2027. EHR vendors must certify for interop/security by Jan 2026. Priority health data exchange (patient summaries, ePrescriptions) mandatory EU-wide by March 2029.

**EU MDR / GDPR:** MDR applies if Fractal outputs constitute clinical decision support (Class IIa/IIb). GDPR special-category health rules → consent management + DPIA — direct NEOS revenue opportunity.

## 6. Procurement Signals

**Buyer personas:** CIO (infra, vendor consolidation, integration), CMIO (workflow, patient safety, physician adoption), CDO (data strategy, AI governance), CCO (HIPAA, HITRUST, GDPR). Pharma: VP Data Science, Head of RWE, CTO.

**Capital committee:** IT capital >$1M requires board/capital committee. Decision: 6–18 months. EHR replacement: 5–10-yr cycles. Analytics/interop refresh: 3–5 yrs. Budget cycles Jul–Sep; Q4/Q1 peak signing.

**RFP cadence:** 68% of providers manage procurement manually (2024 HIMSS). RFPs: 6–12 mo issuance to award. Evaluation committees: 3–5 hrs/wk per member; technical fit weighted 30–35%; regulatory compliance gating (pass/fail).

**Vendor risk mgmt:** HITRUST cert attachment gating before submission. BAA + SOC 2 Type II + pen test standard. CISOs require AI governance docs for any ML/AI platform.

---

## 7. Rejection Reasons

**Patient safety risk:** Any clinical-workflow touch faces CMIO/patient-safety officer scrutiny. Position as decision-support, not autonomous; pursue Class I/II SaMD pathway early.

**HIPAA fear:** Privacy officers default-reject when PHI flows unclear. Document de-identification methodology, BAA scope, subprocessor chains BEFORE any technical demo.

**"Rip and replace" resistance:** Hospitals that spent $300M–$1.2B on Epic are committed. Position AMP+AZOA as layered augmentation — "We wrap Epic, we don't replace it."

**Epic/Cerner integration lock-in:** Epic proprietary APIs (MyChart, Hyperdrive, App Orchard) create real friction. 6–18 mo Epic technical review + App Orchard certification fees. AZOA must achieve Epic FHIR R4 cert + App Orchard listing.

**Procurement cycle mismatch:** 12–18 mo RFPs disadvantage startups. Lack of segment references = hard rejection. Lighthouse program (2–3 references per community/regional/national) prerequisite.

## Summary

Healthcare is PrimusNeo's highest-complexity, highest-value vertical: 3-yr deals from $1.5M (community hospital) to $200M+ (national IDN / top-20 pharma). Epic's gravitational dominance + multi-billion-$ integration-services tax + regulatory stack (HIPAA, HITRUST, TEFCA, EHDS) function as moats for certified vendors. Per-bed and per-provider dominate EHR; PMPM dominates payer/population health; ELAs close largest pharma/IDN deals. HITRUST i1 non-negotiable before substantive RFP. Winning motion: wrap, don't replace Epic/Cerner — AMP for spatial ops intelligence, AZOA as TEFCA-native FHIR fabric, NEOS for consent/data governance, Fractal for RWE + population analytics. CIOs + CMIOs co-buy; patient safety + HIPAA anxiety are primary emotional rejection levers — neutralize in discovery convo #1.
