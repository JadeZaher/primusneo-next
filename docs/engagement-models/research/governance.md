## 2. Engagement Scale Tiers — 3-Year TCO

### Small: 1 Town / 1 Cooperative / 1 Small DAO (<$10M treasury)
- Software $20K–$80K/yr (civic engagement + basic board portal + identity)
- Impl $15K–$60K one-time
- Services $0–$50K (self-service or boutique)
- Compliance: StateRAMP-lite or none; basic SSI; no FedRAMP
- **3-Year TCO: $75K–$300K**

### Mid: City 100K–500K / State Agency / Mid DAO ($10M–$500M treasury)
- Software $80K–$400K/yr (GovTech ERP + engagement + spatial + board portal + identity fabric)
- Impl $250K–$1.5M one-time
- Services $200K–$800K/yr (Deloitte GPS or regional SI)
- Compliance: FedRAMP Moderate or StateRAMP/GovRAMP; GDPR if EU
- **3-Year TCO: $1.2M–$5M**

### Large: State-Level / Federal Agency / G20 Megacity / Top-50 DAO ($500M+ treasury)
- Software $500K–$5M+/yr (Esri statewide EA + Tyler ERP + Granicus state suite + identity fabric)
- Impl $2M–$20M+ (multi-year, multi-team program)
- Services $2M–$15M/yr (Big-4 or Booz Allen PM)
- Compliance: FedRAMP High or DoD IL4/IL5; MiCA CASP licensing for EU DAOs

## 4. Pricing Mechanics

**Per-resident:** Granicus, OpenGov, CitizenLab, Bang the Table use informal population-banded pricing. City of 50K residents ~$25K/yr; 500K ~$120K/yr for comparable modules. Not published; negotiated.

**Per-member / per-user:** Board portals (Diligent, Boardvantage) + enterprise SaaS charge per seat. SSI platforms per credential tx. Tyler SaaS courts $125–$370/user/mo.

**Per-vote / per-tx:** Snapshot free (off-chain). Tally + on-chain Governor: gas pass-through $0.01–$50/vote depending on chain + congestion. Entra Verified ID per-tx above 50K/mo threshold.

**% of treasury:** Karpatkey, Llama, comparable DAO treasury managers charge **0.25–0.75% AUM** or fixed retainer. $100M treasury → $250K–$750K/yr before performance component.

**Enterprise license:** Esri statewide EAs, Tyler ERP, Granicus state contracts = flat annual against population counts + user volumes — typically **$500K–$5M+/yr at state scale**.

**Grant / public funding:** Decidim, Consul, Pol.is — OSS with deployment costs borne by agency. EU Horizon funds + US federal innovation grants regularly fund participatory democracy deployments.

## 5. Regulatory Overlay

| Framework | Applies To | Cost Implication |
|-----------|-----------|------------------|
| **FedRAMP Moderate** | US federal SaaS | **$400K–$900K to authorize**; $100K–$300K/yr ongoing |
| **FedRAMP High** | Highly sensitive federal data | 450+ controls; JAB or agency ATO; **$2M–$5M+ authorization** |
| **StateRAMP / GovRAMP** | US state + local (rebranded 2025); no FedRAMP reciprocity | $150K–$500K |
| **DoD IL2** | Non-sensitive DoD public data | ~325 controls; FedRAMP Mod equivalent; $400K–$800K |
| **DoD IL4** | CUI + contingency ops | Add'l DoD-specific controls beyond IL2; $800K–$2M+ |
| **DoD IL5** | NSS data + higher-sensitivity CUI | 450+ controls; restricted DISA-authorized cloud only; **$2M–$5M+** |
| **GDPR / EU NIS2** | EU public sector | Data residency in EEA; DPA; DPIA; privacy by design; up to €20M or 4% global turnover in fines |
| **MiCA (EU)** | DAOs + CASPs operating in EU | CASP licensing required by end-2024 (stablecoins from June 2024); 18-mo grandfathering ending mid-2026; **legal + licensing €200K–€1M+** |
| **SAB 121 / SAB 122** | US DAO + crypto custodians | SAB 121 rescinded Jan 2025 via SAB 122; custodied crypto no longer on custodian balance sheet — reduces custody compliance overhead |
| **CMMC / CMMI** | Defense-adjacent contractors | Maturity Levels 1–3; Level 3 requires C3PAO assessment; **$500K–$3M+** |

## 6. Procurement Signals

### Buyer Personas

| Persona | Org Type | Trigger |
|---------|----------|---------|
| **CIO / CITO** | City, county, state, federal | Modernization mandate; legacy EOL; federal efficiency pressure |
| **Chief Innovation Officer** | Large metro, state, federal | Pilot/innovation budget; often outside traditional IT procurement |
| **City Manager / County Admin** | Local gov | Budget cycle; council mandate; constituent service pressure |
| **DAO Steward Council / Foundation** | Protocol DAO | Token holder vote; treasury diversification; ops maturity milestone |
| **Cooperative Board / Federation Steward** | Worker co-op, credit union, housing co-op | Board vote; governance reform; federation membership requirement |

### Cooperative + Expedited Procurement Vehicles

- **Sourcewell:** Pre-competed cooperative; 50,000+ member agencies. **Ideal first cooperative vehicle for PrimusNeo targeting US local government**
- **NASPO ValuePoint:** Lead-state model; all 50 states + DC + territories eligible. Strong for cloud/SaaS. Separate enrollment from Sourcewell
- **GSA MAS Schedule (SIN 518210C for IT; OASIS+ for prof services):** Federal + state/local cooperative purchasing authority built into contract clauses
- **State-specific schedules:** TX DIR, CA DGS, NY OGS — non-negotiated access to pre-priced agreements within those states

### Process Norms

- Government procurement cycles: **6–18 months RFP to award** for mid/large. Small/micro-purchase thresholds (under $250K federal) allow sole-source + streamlined
- **DAO governance procurement:** Token holder vote via Snapshot signal → Tally on-chain execution. Full proposal-to-execution cycle: **2–8 weeks** incl discussion period
- Budget cycles: Federal FY Oct 1. State/local vary; most decisions compress into Q3–Q4. Missing a budget cycle = 12-month delay

## 7. Rejection Reasons

| Risk | Driver |
|------|--------|
| **Procurement red tape** | New vendor not enrolled on approved cooperative vehicle or agency-specific approved list. Incumbent (Tyler, Granicus, Esri) lock-in with switching costs $500K–$5M+ |
| **FedRAMP gap** | No ATO = automatic disqualification for most US federal + many state agencies. **FedRAMP 20x initiative (launched 2025) aims to accelerate via agency-path in ~90 days** using pre-authorized infra boundaries |
| **DoD security classification gap** | IL4/IL5 block commercially-hosted SaaS without DISA provisional. CMMC Level 2–3 required for defense-adjacent |
| **DAO treasury security** | **$4B+ lost to governance attacks + treasury exploits since 2016**. Multi-sig thresholds commonly too low (2/3 cited as insufficient; experts recommend min 3/5 for <$10M, 4/7 for larger) |
| **Governance attack vectors** | Sybil attacks on voting identity, delegation abuse, flash-loan voting power acquisition. MiCA regulators cite these as institutional adoption barriers. **Governance attack = existential risk for DAO clients** |
| **Data sovereignty / GDPR** | EU public sector requires data residency in EEA. US-headquartered vendor without EU hosting = automatic disqualifier in most EU public tenders |
| **Proof of scale / references** | Governments require **3–5 comparable-agency references**. Early-stage vendors without marquee public-sector references routinely eliminated in technical evaluation |
| **Budget cycle mismatch** | Missing a gov's annual budget decision window = 12-month pipeline delay regardless of product fit |

## Summary

Governance spans two structurally different buyer archetypes — traditional public-sector agencies + emergent Web3 organizations — but both share core need for credible, auditable, multi-stakeholder decision infrastructure at scale. **GovTech incumbents (Tyler, Granicus, OpenGov, Esri) command $20K–$5M+ annual contracts** and benefit from cooperative purchasing lock-in but face criticism for cost overruns, slow delivery, poor interop. Participatory democracy tools (Decidim, CitizenLab) occupy lower-cost lane ($0–$80K/yr) but lack enterprise orchestration. **DAO governance infra (Snapshot, Tally, Aragon) free at protocol layer; economic value concentrates in treasury management services ($200K–$2.5M/yr)** where Llama + Karpatkey define pricing benchmark at 0.25–0.75% AUM or fixed retainer. PrimusNeo's combo (AMP + NEOS + AZOA + Fractal) positions above point solutions, below full SI programs. Primary barriers: **FedRAMP/StateRAMP compliance ($400K–$900K, 12–24 mo), cooperative purchasing vehicle enrollment, proof-of-scale references**. DAO adoption barriers: governance security architecture + MiCA licensing overhead in EU. **Optimal entry: Sourcewell or NASPO enrollment + a single marquee city or mid-DAO reference deployment to unlock broader pipeline.**
