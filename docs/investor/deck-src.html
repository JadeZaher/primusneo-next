    01 — The problemWhy good work goes unbuilt

    A grower knows where the food spoils.

    Good ideas die in conversation.

    Two states away, an engineer has built the scheduler that would save that food. A buyer would pay for the difference. The three never share a table. The idea is spoken, agreed with and left in the room, and next season the food spoils in the same place.

    02 — The labClearing house + innovation lab

    Useful results earn the next step.

    A clearing house finds the people. A lab finds the proof.

    PrimusNeo matches a real need with the knowledge and tools that can meet it. Elevate Impact, our research partner, turns the question into a study the evidence can answer. The lab builds a first version, tests it against measures agreed in advance, and lets the evidence choose what happens next. Every project leaves relationships, records and reusable tools behind, so the next one starts with more.

    1. 01Understand the need

      The people who live with the problem describe it.

    2. 02Form the team

      Knowledge, tools and a delivery partner, each one named.

    3. 03Define the test

      The question and its measures, set before anything is built.

    4. 04Build a first version

      Existing tools where they fit. New ones where they don't.

    5. 05Review the evidence

      Against the baseline, in the open.

    6. 06Put it to work

      Expand it, adapt it, or stop.

    Four ways a project ends well: research people can use · a tool or service · a lasting partnership · a new company. “You cannot jump to the end. That is the point.”

    04 — The railsAZOA · open-source settlement engine

    A workflow you can trust with money.

    Value moves once, or not at all.

    AZOA is the open engine under the stack's money. It carries a multi-step financial workflow to completion across blockchains and fiat. When a result is ambiguous, it waits for the truth. When a workflow is stuck, it hands the decision to a person, with the whole story attached.

    Exactly once

    A retried or duplicated step settles once, or not at all. Never twice.

    Fail closed

    An unverified signature or a lapsed consent grant refuses the action.

    Reconcile, never guess

    Status comes from the chain or the settlement partner before the next irreversible step.

    Escalate to a person

    A stuck workflow is flagged for manual intervention, never quietly resolved.

    10 — How it earnsPricing & unit economics

    Everyone who does the work gets paid.

    Two prices. Both paid for finished work.

    Most of the money arrives as work is delivered: a patient enrolled, a study designed, a paper finished. The deployment fee opens the door. The units are the business.

    Option A · platform access $80,000

    per organization, per year. Access to the platform and its tools without a delivery engagement.

    Option B · platform + delivery $250K – $400K

    per deployment, plus $20,000 per agreed billable unit: a patient, a design, a paper. Access is included and never charged twice.

    17 — Why

    Somewhere a town has learned to live with a problem. Somewhere else, a person holds the tool that would end it.

    The distance between them is the whole of our business. We close it with a first test, a fair agreement and a ledger that pays everyone who did the work. When the evidence is good, we build. When it is not, we stop and say so.

    That is how the lab works. It is also how a decent society works.
